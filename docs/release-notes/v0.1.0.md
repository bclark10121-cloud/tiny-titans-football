# Tiny Titans Football v0.1.0

First test build.

## New in this version

- 11 on 11 exhibition game against the computer. Four 2-minute quarters.
- 8 offensive plays and 4 defenses with a play call screen.
- Passing, running, juke, dive, tackle and switch defender.
- Field goals and extra points with a timing meter. Auto punt on fourth down.
- 4 fictional teams. Practice mode.
- Keyboard, controller and touch controls.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page. Tap Play in your browser. The title card shows.
2. Press Start. Pick a team. The play call screen shows 8 plays.
3. Call Quick Slants. Snap. Throw to receiver 1. Run after the catch.
4. Call Sweep Right. Snap. Sprint, juke and dive.
5. Reach fourth down. Punt and Field Goal cards appear.
6. Score a touchdown. Stop the kick marker in the green for the extra point.
7. Play defense. Pick Blitz. Switch defender and tackle the ball carrier.
8. Open Menu. Every option shows in a row. Sound toggles. How to play opens.
9. Finish a game. The final score, yards and your record show.
10. On a phone: install the APK. Drag on the left to move. Use the buttons on the right. Tap a receiver to pass.
11. With a controller: left stick moves, A snaps, face buttons pass.

## Known limits

- Tested in headless Chrome and by a test bot. Not tested on a real phone or controller yet.
- The APK is signed with a test key. A later build may need this one uninstalled first.
- No kicker on kicks, no jersey numbers and no returns yet.
