# Tiny Titans Football v0.2.0

Second test build. The full kicking game, a bigger playbook, option runs and auto pass.

## New in this version

- Kickoffs and punts you play. Stop the marker in the green, then cover the kick.
- Kick and punt returns. Interception and fumble returns. Touchbacks, muffed punts and safeties.
- Two-point tries, sudden death overtime and a halftime kickoff.
- 33 offensive plays on four tabs: Short, Deep, Run and Option. 20 defenses on four tabs.
- Option plays: Speed Option Right and Left, Pistol Option, Zone Read and Zone Read Left.
- New runs: Counter and Stretch Right.
- Auto pass setting: the QB throws for you and you take over the catcher.
- Hot routes and audibles before the snap. Tap a receiver for a bullet, hold for a lob.
- Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty.
- Jersey numbers. A holder and kicker on field goals.
- Smoother on phones: players draw in 10 batches, the frame rate is capped at 60 and the screen respects notches.
- Saves survive closing the app.

## Install

The APK uses the same test key as v0.1.0. It installs over v0.1.0. No uninstall needed.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page. Tap Play in your browser. The title card shows.
2. Press Start. The team screen shows Difficulty, Practice mode and Auto pass. Pick Normal and a team.
3. If your team kicks off: the kicker, the whole kicking line and the meter all show. Stop the marker in the green. Run down and tackle the returner.
4. If you receive: catch the kick and run it back. Sprint with Shift or Space, A on a controller or the Sprint button.
5. On offense, check all four tabs with Q and E (or LB and RB). The Option tab shows 5 plays with diagrams.
6. Call Speed Option Right. Snap. Run at the edge. Press E (or B, or the Pitch button) to pitch. You now control the running back.
7. Call Speed Option Right again and keep it. Run it yourself.
8. Call Zone Read. Snap. The banner says "QB keeps" or "Hand off". You control whoever has the ball.
9. Call Counter and Stretch Right from the Run tab.
10. Before a pass play snap: press 1 to change receiver 1's route. Press R for an audible.
11. Snap a pass play. Tap 1 for a bullet. On another play, hold 1 for a lob.
12. Open Menu. Six options show in one row. Turn Auto pass on. The game pauses while the Menu is open.
13. Call a pass play with Auto pass on. Snap. The QB throws by himself. After a catch, you control the receiver.
14. Turn Auto pass off in the Menu. Your next pass play is yours to throw again.
15. Reach fourth down deep in your end. The Kick tab opens. Punt. The punter shows with the meter above him.
16. Reach field goal range. Kick a field goal. The holder, kicker and posts all show.
17. Drive from inside your own 10. The near goal posts do not block the quarterback.
18. Check jersey numbers. They sit on the players' backs and read the right way. Nothing floats.
19. Score a touchdown. Pick the extra point or go for two.
20. Play defense. Pick a Blitz. Switch defender with Tab. Tackle the ball carrier.
21. Tie after four quarters goes to sudden death overtime.
22. Close the app or tab mid-game, open it again. Your record and settings are still there.
23. On a phone: install the APK over v0.1.0. Drag on the left to move. Use the buttons on the right. Tap a receiver to pass.
24. With a controller: left stick moves, A snaps, face buttons pass, B pitches on option plays.

## What was tested

- A test bot played 6 full games headless with every change. Every game finished. Scores ran from 0 to 30 a side.
- The browser test played part of a game in headless Chrome and printed `errors []`. At most 36 draw calls, about 5,700 triangles and 44 MB of video memory.
- Auto pass: 8 plays in practice mode in Chrome. The QB threw on all 8. On each catch, control moved to the receiver.
- Kickoff, punt and field goal camera checked by screenshot in Chrome.

## Known limits

- Not tested on a real phone or controller yet.
- Season mode moved to v0.3.0.
- The APK is signed with a test key. A later build with Brian's own key will need this one uninstalled first.
- In the software renderer used for testing, some jersey numbers floated off the players. The hardware renderer drew them on the players' backs. Please check item 18 on your devices.
