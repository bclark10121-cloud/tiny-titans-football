# Tiny Titans Football v0.2.0

Second test build. 7 on 7 is the new default game. Full Xbox controller support. The whole kicking game, a bigger playbook and option runs.

## New in this version

- 7 on 7 is the default. Offense: 3 linemen, a QB, a running back and 2 receivers. Defense: 3 linemen, 2 linebackers and 2 defensive backs. Same full field.
- 11 on 11 is still there. Pick Players on the team screen or in the Menu.
- Full Xbox controller support. Every screen works with only a controller. A chooses and B goes back. View or Menu opens the Menu.
- Kickoffs and punts you play. Stop the marker in the green, then cover the kick.
- Kick and punt returns. Interception and fumble returns. Touchbacks, muffed punts and safeties.
- Two-point tries, sudden death overtime and a halftime kickoff.
- 33 offensive plays on four tabs: Short, Deep, Run and Option. 20 defenses on four tabs.
- Option plays: Speed Option Right and Left, Pistol Option, Zone Read and Zone Read Left.
- New runs: Counter and Stretch Right.
- Auto pass setting: the QB throws for you and you take over the catcher.
- Hot routes and audibles before the snap. Tap a receiver's button for a bullet, hold it for a lob.
- Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty.
- Jersey numbers. A holder and kicker on field goals.
- Smoother on phones: players draw in 10 batches, the frame rate is capped at 60 and the screen respects notches.
- Saves survive closing the app. A damaged save no longer stops the game.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.5.0 (commit b2c2865). It brings the shared save store, the test switch gate and the controller setup.

## Install

If you installed v0.1.0, uninstall it once first. v0.1.0 used a test signature. v0.2.0 uses the real signing key, so later versions install over it.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page. Tap Play in your browser. The title card shows.
2. Press Start. The team screen shows Difficulty, Players (7 on 7 picked), Practice mode and Auto pass. Pick Normal and a team.
3. Count the players. Your offense has 7: 3 linemen, a QB, a running back and 2 receivers. The defense has 7.
4. If your team kicks off: the kicker, the kicking line and the meter all show. Stop the marker in the green. Run down and tackle the returner.
5. If you receive: catch the kick and run it back. Sprint with Shift or Space, RT on a controller or the Sprint button.
6. On offense, check all four tabs with Q and E (or LB and RB). The Option tab shows 5 plays with diagrams.
7. Call Speed Option Right. Snap. Run at the edge. Press E (or B, or the Pitch button) to pitch. You now control the running back.
8. Call Zone Read. Snap. The banner says "QB keeps" or "Hand off". You control whoever has the ball.
9. Call a pass play. Before the snap, press 1 to change receiver 1's route. Press R for an audible.
10. Snap a pass play. Tap 1 for a bullet. On another play, hold 1 for a lob.
11. Open Menu. Switch Players to 11 on 11. The next play has 11 a side. Switch back to 7 on 7.
12. Turn Auto pass on in the Menu. Call a pass play. The QB throws by himself. After a catch, you control the receiver.
13. Reach fourth down deep in your end. The Kick tab opens. Punt. The punter shows with the meter above him.
14. Reach field goal range. Kick a field goal. The holder, kicker and posts all show.
15. Check jersey numbers. They sit on the players' backs and read the right way. Nothing floats.
16. Score a touchdown. Pick the extra point or go for two.
17. Play defense. Pick a Blitz. Switch defender with Tab. Tackle the ball carrier.
18. Close the app or tab mid-game, open it again. Your record and settings are still there.
19. Xbox controller, browser or phone: pair it (steps on the game page). Put the touch screen and keyboard away.
20. With only the controller: A starts, the stick picks a team, A chooses. LB and RB change play tabs. A picks a play and A snaps.
21. On a pass play, each receiver shows A, B, X or Y. Tap that button for a bullet. Hold it for a lob.
22. B jukes, or pitches on an option play. X dives or tackles. LB switches defender. Y calls an audible.
23. Press View or Menu. The Menu opens. B closes it. Help also closes with B.
24. On a phone: uninstall v0.1.0 first, then install v0.2.0. Drag on the left to move. Use the buttons on the right. Tap a receiver to pass.

## What was tested

- A test bot played 6 full 7 on 7 games and 6 full 11 on 11 games headless. Every game finished. 7 on 7 scores ran from 17 to 47 a side. 11 on 11 scores ran from 0 to 21.
- The browser test played part of a game in headless Chrome and printed `errors []`. At most 34 draw calls, about 5,500 triangles and 44 MB of video memory.
- A pretend Xbox controller in Chrome went from the title to a thrown pass with only the controller, then opened and closed the Menu with View, Menu and B. No errors.
- Two headless checks drove the title, the team screen, the Players choice, play calls, bullets and lobs, the kick meter, the Menu, How to play and the end of game screen with controller buttons only. Both passed.
- Test switches checked: without `?test` the page has no test hooks, and `?bot` or `?fast` do nothing.
- The shared save check passed. It feeds the save damaged, cut-off and oversized files and checks each one falls back to safe values.
- The APK was signed with the real key and its signature checked.
- Kickoff, play call and pass screens checked by screenshot in Chrome.

## Known limits

- Not tested on a real phone or a real controller yet.
- 7 on 7 scores run higher than 11 on 11. Tell us if games feel too high scoring.
- The 2 second speed burst button moved to v0.3.0. Season mode also moved to v0.3.0.
- In the software renderer used for some tests, jersey numbers sometimes floated off the players. The hardware renderer drew them on the players' backs. Please check item 15 on your devices.
