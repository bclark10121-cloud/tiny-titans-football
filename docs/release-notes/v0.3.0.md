# Tiny Titans Football v0.3.0

Third test build. Season mode, the burst button and seven quality of life fixes.

## New in this version

- Season mode. Pick a team and play 6 weeks against the other 3 teams, twice each. The other game each week is played for you. The top 2 meet in the Title Game. Standings save between visits.
- Burst. 2 seconds of top speed, ready again 5 seconds after it ends. F on a keyboard, LT on a controller, the Burst button on touch. Hold-to-sprint stays on Shift, RT and the Sprint button.
- Continue. The game saves at every play call, kickoff and try. Close the app or go to the title, then pick the game up from the Continue button.
- Settings. One panel for Music, Sound effects, Vibration, Auto pass, Players (7 on 7 or 11 on 11) and Quarter length (1, 2 or 3 minutes). Open it from the title card or the Menu.
- Run it again. The play call opens on your last play, marked Last. One press calls it again. Fourth down still opens the Kick tab.
- Skip the wait. After the whistle, A, Space or a tap moves on once half a second has passed.
- Pause on focus loss. If the app or browser tab loses focus mid-game, the Menu opens and the clock stops.
- Vibration. Tackles buzz the phone and rumble the controller. Sacks and fumbles buzz harder. Turn it off in Settings.
- Clearer team screen. Practice mode and Auto pass show On or Off in words.
- B goes back on every screen: Settings, How to play, the Menu, the team screen, the season screen and the end screen.
- The D-pad stays on the play cards, and the play card comes back into focus when the Menu closes.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.5.0 (commit b2c2865). No kit change in this release.

## Install

v0.3.0 installs over v0.2.0. If you still have v0.1.0, uninstall it first. It used a test signature.

The APK asks for one new permission: vibration.

## Test checklist

1. Open the game page. Tap Play in your browser. The title card shows Exhibition, Season and Settings.
2. Open Settings. Turn Music off. The title music stops. Turn it back on.
3. Turn Sound effects off, start a game and check the crowd and hits are silent. Turn them back on.
4. Set Quarter length to 1 minute. Start an exhibition. The clock starts at 1:00. Set it back to 2.
5. On the team screen, Practice mode and Auto pass say On or Off. B or Back returns to the title.
6. Start an exhibition. Call a play. On the next play call, the same play is highlighted and marked Last.
7. After a play ends, press A, Space or tap. The next play call comes up right away.
8. On a run, press the burst: F, LT or the Burst button. The runner speeds up for 2 seconds. The Burst button fills a gold ring while it charges for 5 seconds.
9. On defense, the burst works for the defender you control too.
10. Mid-game, switch to another app or tab. Come back. The Menu is open and the clock has not moved.
11. Mid-game, open the Menu and pick Title. The title card shows Continue with the score. Press Continue. The game picks up at the play call.
12. Close the app mid-game. Open it again. Continue is there.
13. Make a tackle on a phone. The phone buzzes. Turn Vibration off in Settings and check it stops.
14. With an Xbox controller, make a tackle. The controller rumbles.
15. Pick Season on the title card. Pick a team. The season screen shows week 1 of 6 and this week's rival.
16. Play week 1. At the end, press Season. The standings show your result and the other game of the week.
17. Go to the title. The Season button says Season: week 2. Press it to get back to the season screen.
18. Play through the season. If you finish in the top 2, you play the Title Game. If not, it is played for you.
19. A tied Title Game keeps going into more overtime until someone scores.
20. Press End season twice to end a season early.
21. With only a controller: the D-pad and A reach every button on the title card, Settings, the team screen and the season screen. B goes back from each.
22. Open How to play. Both columns fit on the screen.
23. On a phone: install over v0.2.0. Your record and settings are still there.

## What was tested

- A test bot played 6 full 7 on 7 games and 6 full 11 on 11 games headless. Every game finished. 7 on 7 scores ran from 13 to 41 a side. 11 on 11 scores ran from 0 to 28.
- A test bot played a whole season headless: 6 weeks and the Title Game. The season ended with a champion.
- A new headless check passed 30 of 30 steps. It covers Settings, season screens, Continue, Run it again, skip the wait, pause on focus loss, the burst, the save checks and quarter length. Menus were driven with controller buttons only.
- The screens check and the controller check passed with the new Menu row.
- The shared save check passed. The new check also loads damaged standings and a damaged saved game. Both are set aside.
- The browser test played part of a game in Chrome and printed `errors []`. It opened Settings and a season from the title, toggled Auto pass in Settings and used the burst. At most 36 draw calls, about 5,600 triangles and 45 MB of video memory.
- A pretend Xbox controller in Chrome opened Settings, started a season, played a pass and used LT for the burst. `errors []`.
- Test switches checked: without `?test` the page has no test hooks, and `?bot` does nothing.
- The APK was signed with the real key and its signature, version and vibration permission checked.

## Known limits

- Not tested on a real phone or a real controller yet. Vibration and rumble were never felt by a person.
- Season games against teams you do not play are simulated from team ratings, not played out.
- A season is always 6 weeks. Tell us if it should be shorter or longer.
- Browsers differ on vibration. Chrome on Android buzzes. Desktop browsers mostly do not. Controller rumble in a browser is untested.
- In the software renderer used for some tests, jersey numbers sometimes floated off the players. The hardware renderer drew them on the players' backs.
