# Tiny Titans Football v0.3.5

Sound effects work in the browser. Until now every sound effect was silent in the browser: the whistle, kicks, hits, cheers and the menu clicks. Only the music played. Android was not affected.

## Five minute test

1. Open the game page on a computer and press Play in the browser. Click once on the game. The title music starts.
2. Start a game. The whistle blows and the kicks, hits and cheers come through.
3. Open Menu, then Settings. Set Music to Off. The music stops and the sound effects keep playing. Set Music back to Full.
4. Set Sound to Off. The sound effects stop and the music keeps playing. Set Sound back to Full.
5. Install the APK over v0.3.4. Sounds play on Android as before.

## New in this version

- Every sound effect now plays in the browser. The game's sound effects channel had no route to the speakers, and browsers drop a channel with no route. The music channel had one, so the music always played. The v0.3.4 sound fix brought the music back but missed this.
- The sound test now also checks that every sound channel has a route to the speakers.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.8.2. No kit change.

## Install

Version 0.3.5 installs over 0.3.4 and keeps your saves and settings.

## Full test checklist

1. Browser: the whistle, kicks, hits and cheers play during a game, and the menus click.
2. Browser: Music Off in Settings leaves the sound effects playing. Sound Off leaves the music playing.
3. Browser: the music plays as in v0.3.4.
4. Android: every sound plays as before.
