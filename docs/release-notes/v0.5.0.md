# Tiny Titans Football v0.5.0

New players. Every player now matches the chunky toy look from the approved Gemini art: big helmet, face mask, huge pads and mitten gloves. They run with real knee and arm motion and celebrate touchdowns.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.4.0. Your saves stay.
2. Press Play, pick a slot and start an Exhibition game.
3. At the kickoff, look at the players up close: a rounded helmet in the team colour, a charcoal face mask, a face with eyes, big rounded shoulder pads, bare arms, big charcoal gloves, sock bands and cleats. Numbers sit on the chest and back.
4. Take the ball and run. Legs fold at the knee, arms pump and the runner leans forward. Hit the burst: the lean grows.
5. Score a touchdown. Watch your team celebrate: jumps, flexes, spins and a high-knee dance.
6. Try a field goal. The holder kneels on one knee.

## New in this version

- New player look built to match the approved front, side and back views. Low-poly faceted shapes, like the reference art.
- Team colours: helmet, jersey, pads, sock bands and pants. Face mask, gloves and cleats stay charcoal for every team.
- Better run cycle: knee fold, arm pump with bent elbows, forward lean and a bob each stride. Blockers lean in with arms out.
- Four touchdown celebrations: jump, flex, spin and dance. Each player has one.
- The game still draws every player on the field in 16 draw calls, so it stays smooth on phones.
