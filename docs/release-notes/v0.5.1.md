# Tiny Titans Football v0.5.1

New music. "Stage 3", a retro arcade sports track, replaces "Rock on Chicago". BC picked it from three options.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.5.0. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game and tap or press any button. The new music starts: a chiptune sports beat.
3. Listen for about a minute. The track loops with no gap or click.
4. Open Settings and change Music from 4 down to 1, then back. The volume follows.
5. Open Settings, then About. The credit reads "Stage 3" by Juhani Junkala.

## New in this version

- New title music: "Stage 3" by Juhani Junkala (CC0, public domain). A 60-second loop.
- Loudness matched to the old track, so your Music setting sounds the same.
- Smaller file: 0.7 MB instead of 1.4 MB.
- Credits updated in the game, on the game page and in CREDITS.md.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.11.0 (no change).

## Install

Version 0.5.1 installs over 0.5.0 and keeps your saves.
