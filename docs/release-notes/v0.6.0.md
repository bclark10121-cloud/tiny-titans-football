# Tiny Titans Football v0.6.0

Back moves to the top and the game plays both ways. Every full-screen menu has Back at the top left, so Android's bottom bar and notifications never cover it. Hold the phone upright or sideways: menus and gameplay fit either way. This is the pilot for two new standards (G5.30 and G6.18) and kit 1.13.0.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.5.2. Your saves stay.
2. Hold the phone sideways. Open Settings. Back sits at the top left. Scroll the list: Back stays put.
3. Turn the phone upright. Settings redraws in one column at full size. Back is still at the top left.
4. Close Settings. Press Play. The slot list, the play mode screen, the team screen and How to play all have Back at the top left.
5. Start a game upright. The camera shows more field ahead and behind. The play cards show three across.
6. Turn the phone mid-play. The game keeps going and the screen redraws.
7. With a controller, press up from the first row of any menu. Focus reaches Back.

## New in this version

- Back at the top left of Settings, the slot list, the play mode screen, the team screen, How to play, Records and Change controls (G5.30). The header with Back never scrolls. B, Esc and the Android back gesture still close.
- Portrait play (G6.18). Menus keep their full size upright. The team list stacks in one column, play cards show three across, the Menu row moves under the scoreboard and long banners wrap.
- The camera keeps a fixed side-to-side view upright and shows more of the field.
- Turning the device mid-game keeps your game.
- How to play text is larger (18 instead of 15) and scrolls under the header when it is long.
- New five-size browser test: phone and tablet both ways, plus desktop.

## Shared kit

Built with kit 1.13.0 (new: `panel_header.gd` and `screen_fit.gd`).

## Install

Version 0.6.0 installs over 0.5.2 and keeps your saves. Android's rotation lock still applies: unlock rotation to turn the game.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Phone sideways and upright: Settings, slot list, team screen, How to play.
- [ ] A full drive upright: play call, pass, run, kick.
- [ ] Turn the phone mid-play.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: every quick check, the old save check, 12 computer-played games (6 in 7 on 7, 6 in 11 on 11), the browser walkthrough, Settings, review, controller, Tester tools and sound tests, and the new five-size test (phone 844 x 390 and 390 x 844, tablet 1180 x 820 and 820 x 1180, desktop 1280 x 720) with a turn in the middle of a play. Screenshots at every size were checked by eye. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate, no internet permission and the turn-with-the-phone setting.

Not tested: a real phone, the Retroid or a real controller.
