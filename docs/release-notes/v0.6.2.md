# Tiny Titans Football v0.6.2

Match music. A game now plays its own song under the crowd, different from the start screen song.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.6.1. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game on Android. The title track, "Stage 3", starts with no tap.
3. Start an Exhibition game. At kickoff the title track stops and "Funk Game Loop" plays under the crowd.
4. Open the pause menu and quit to the title. "Stage 3" comes back.
5. Turn Music off in Settings. Both songs stay silent. The crowd still plays on the Sound level.

## New in this version

- Match music (G5.21, asked for by BC): "Funk Game Loop" by Kevin MacLeod plays during a match, under the crowd.
- The start screen, team select and the final score keep the title track.
- Correction: the v0.6.1 notes named the title track "Rock on Chicago". The title track is "Stage 3" by Juhani Junkala, as it has been since v0.5.1.

## Shared kit

Kit 1.13.0, unchanged.

## Install

Version 0.6.2 installs over 0.6.1 and keeps your saves.

## Test checklist

- [ ] Android: title music on the start screen with no tap.
- [ ] Browser: title music after the first tap.
- [ ] Kickoff switches to the match music. The final score switches back.
- [ ] Music Off keeps both songs silent.

## Tested and not tested

Tested on the cloud test machine: a new music check (title on the start screen with no press, match music and crowd in a match, title again after), one full bot game, the save, settings, slots, screens and quality checks, the browser sound check and the browser play test. The signed APK was checked for its signature, the tester gate and no internet permission.

Not tested: a real phone or the Retroid. Listen once on a phone to confirm the switch at kickoff.

## Caveat

In a browser the first tap is still needed before any sound. That is a browser rule and no game can skip it.
