# Tiny Titans Football v0.6.4

Tiny Titans Football now runs on Godot 4.7.2 (G6.19). Nothing about play changes. Your running, passing and tackling notes from the playtest sweep come in their own release once you say what felt off.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.6.3. Your saves stay.
2. Open the game. The title track plays and the start screen shows.
3. Play for a couple of minutes with touch, then with the Xbox controller.
4. Open Settings and the Tester tools. Both open as before.

## Changed

- Godot 4.7.2 (G6.19). No change you should notice.

## Shared kit

Kit unchanged.

## Install

Version 0.6.4 installs over 0.6.3 and keeps your saves.
