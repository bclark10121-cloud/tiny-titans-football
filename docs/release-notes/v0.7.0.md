# Tiny Titans Football v0.7.0

Quick game mode is new: offense only, about five minutes a game. The season screen gains Sim this week. The game also moves to the newest shared kit, with full screen by default in the browser, no 60 frame cap and wide screens filled edge to edge.

## Five minute test

1. Install the APK over v0.6.4. Your saves stay.
2. Press Play, pick a slot, then pick Quick game. It sits between Exhibition and Season. You should start on offense at your own 25 with no kickoff.
3. Score or punt. You should see one gold line such as "Comets drive: 7 plays, 58 yards, touchdown", then get the ball back at your own 25. A, Space or a tap skips the line.
4. Finish the game. It should take about five minutes and the end screen should say Quick game.
5. Start a Season and press Sim this week twice. The week should finish with no game played.
6. In the browser, the first click should take the game full screen. On your phone, play should look smoother at 120 frames a second.

## New

- Quick game. You play every drive on offense, plus your own field goals. Punts are not played: the other team starts 40 yards downfield. The other team's drives are simulated from team strength and the difficulty. Two halves. A tie stands. The result counts in your record.
- Sim this week on the Season screen. It needs a second press. A simulated game counts in the standings only, not in your win and loss record.
- Continue works in a Quick game.

## Changed

- Full screen by default in the browser (G5.11). Turn it off in Settings and it stays off.
- No 60 frame cap (G6.20). The camera, turning and leaning now ease the same at 30, 60 and 120 frames a second.
- 1080p and wider screens are filled edge to edge (G6.21).
- The play mode screen now lists Exhibition, Quick game and Season.

## Judgment calls

1. The scoreboard still says Q1 to Q4 in a Quick game. Halves are two quarters each.
2. A season finished only with Sim this week still earns the season achievements.
3. A missed field goal in a Quick game hands the ball over at the normal spot. A safety starts their drive at their own 25.
4. The Game Shelf picture stays as it is. You pick new card pictures from three options.
5. The start screen title and the play mode panel overlap a little on wide screens. This is older than v0.7.0 and is in the backlog.
